OUR PURPOSE

Making Electricity Usage Easier to Understand

Electricity is an important part of everyday life. Understanding how much energy a household uses can help people plan their expenses and become more aware of their consumption habits.

Our project combines household appliance usage, environmental information and previous electricity consumption to estimate future usage and bill amounts.

The website presents these estimates in a simple format so users can explore their inputs and understand the prediction results.

THE PROCESS

How Our System Works

From household information to an estimated result, the process has four main steps.

01

Enter Information

Provide temperature, humidity, rainfall, appliance usage and previous-month consumption.

02

Process the Data

The application prepares your input in the format expected by the trained machine learning model.

03

Generate Estimates

The models estimate electricity consumption and the corresponding bill amount.

04

Review Results

Review the estimated values and use them to understand possible consumption patterns.
